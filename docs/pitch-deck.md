## Design system (applied to EVERY slide — no exceptions)

**This is the one design. Do not deviate slide to slide.**

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Forest | `#0F6E56` | Primary. Italic emphasis words, CTAs, brand mark, "verified" state |
| Active | `#1D9E75` | Live/real-time state, the pulsing dot, success |
| Mint | `#E1F5EE` | Verified-state tint surfaces only |
| Deep forest | `#0D1F1A` | Dark slides (cover, pivots, close) |
| Off-white | `#F5F4F1` | Default light slide background |
| Paper | `#FAF9F5` | Cards/panels on light slides |
| Ink `#111` / Ink-soft `#2A2A2A` / Muted `#888780` | — | Text hierarchy |
| Alert | `#E24B4A` | "Invalid" state, the "before" world only |

**Type:** Playfair Display (headlines, editorial, 1 italic→forest emphasis word max per headline) · DM Sans (all body) · DM Mono (eyebrows: UPPERCASE, letter-spacing 0.18em, 11px).

**Rules (hard):** No box-shadow. No gradients. Depth = hairlines (0.5px) + contrast + whitespace. Radius 12px cards, 999px pills. One live-dot pulse (active green) — never invent a second animation. Massive whitespace; one idea per slide. Left-aligned editorial grid, not centered clutter.

**Rhythm:** Dark slides bookend and mark pivots (01 Cover · 04 Insight · 09 Future · 13 Vision · 14 Close). Everything else light. This gives the deck a heartbeat.

## 02 · The Problem

- **Surface:** Off-white. Left-aligned, 7-col text / 5-col open.
- **Eyebrow:** `THE PROBLEM`
- **Headline (Playfair):** Unions run the modern workplace on _yesterday's_ tools. _("yesterday's" italic → forest)_
- **Subtext (DM Sans, measure 56ch):** Locals hold enormous responsibility — members' jobs, rights, and dues — yet coordinate it across spreadsheets, paper lists, and email threads that no one fully trusts.
- **Body — 3 hairline-separated lines (DM Mono label + DM Sans line):**
  - `ROSTERS` — Membership lives in a spreadsheet that's out of date the day it's shared.
  - `VERIFICATION` — "Are you a member in good standing?" has no fast, trustworthy answer.
  - `HISTORY` — When something changes, no one can say who changed it, or when.
- **Visual:** A faint, greyed collage of the old world — a spreadsheet grid, a photocopied member list, an email chain — rendered in muted `#888780` at ~15% presence, hairline-framed. Deliberately lifeless.
- **Tone note:** Non-salesy. Name the reality calmly; don't mock the reader.

## 03 · The Cost of Fragmentation

- **Surface:** Paper `#FAF9F5`.
- **Eyebrow:** `THE COST`
- **Headline (Playfair):** Every disconnected system is a _liability_. _(italic → forest)_
- **Subtext:** Fragmentation isn't just inconvenient. For a union, it's exposure — to error, to disputes, to lost trust with members.
- **Body — a "cost ledger" (hairline table, DM Mono figures):**
  | Fragment | What it actually costs |
  |---|---|
  | Spreadsheets | Two versions of the truth; no audit trail |
  | Paper lists | No real-time status; unverifiable at the door |
  | Legacy union CRMs | Vendor lock-in; the union doesn't own its data |
  | Email threads | Decisions with no record; nothing is the source of truth |
  | Manual verification | Slow, subjective, disputable |
  | Disconnected grievance tracking | Cases fall through the cracks |
- **Visual:** Six hairline cards, each a fragment, drifting apart with thin broken 0.5px connector lines that don't quite meet — visual entropy. All neutral/muted; zero green (green is reserved for the solution).
- **One-line stinger (bottom, ink-soft):** The tools don't talk to each other. So the union carries the gaps.

## 06 · Product Today

- **Surface:** Off-white. This slide proves it's real, not vaporware.
- **Eyebrow:** `SHIPPING TODAY`
- **Headline (Playfair):** Already live. Already _yours._ _(italic → forest)_
- **Subtext:** Six systems, working today — the foundation the platform is built on.
- **Body — 6 hairline cards (2×3 grid, DM Mono card-label + one-line DM Sans):**
  1. **Real-time membership** — Live roster; member status updates instantly; always synced; CSV export owned by the union.
  2. **Verification** — Verified or Invalid. Binary, no grey area. QR/scan-based, in real time.
  3. **Digital member card** — Mobile-first, no app to install, always current from the live roster.
  4. **Audit log** — Every change tracked. Who, what, when. Immutable.
  5. **Admin roster dashboard** — Upload CSV, manage status, see active vs lapsed, at the local level.
  6. **Infrastructure principles** — No lock-in, Canadian-hosted, zero tracking, union owns the data.
- **Visual:** Cards flat with 0.5px hairlines; each card carries a tiny DM Mono index (`01`–`06`) top-left. A single active-green live dot on the "Real-time membership" card signals the whole system is live.
- **Credibility chip (footer, DM Mono):** `STATUS: IN PRODUCTION · /api/health → 200`

## 08 · Why It Works

- **Surface:** Paper `#FAF9F5`.
- **Eyebrow:** `WHY IT WORKS`
- **Headline (Playfair):** Built on four things unions can't _compromise._ _(italic → forest)_
- **Body — 4 principles (hairline quadrant, DM Mono numeral + Playfair sub-head + one DM Sans line):**
  1. **Real-time data** — One live truth. What the steward sees is what the executive sees.
  2. **You own your data** — CSV in, CSV out, always. Canadian-hosted. No lock-in, ever.
  3. **Simplicity** — Built for reps, not IT departments. If you can read a roster, you can run it.
  4. **Auditability** — Every change is on the record. Trust comes from transparency, never from taking our word.
- **Visual:** Four equal quadrants divided by a single 0.5px cross-hair. Restraint over decoration. One green accent dot at the intersection.

## 10 · Use Cases

- **Surface:** Off-white.
- **Eyebrow:** `IN THE FIELD`
- **Headline (Playfair):** One system. Every _shift._ _(italic → forest)_
- **Body — 4 workflows (hairline "before → after" rows, DM Mono step labels):**
  1. **Steward workflow** — Before: sticky notes + memory. After: pull up any member, log an interaction, see the history — from your phone.
  2. **Member verification** — Before: "I'll have to check and get back to you." After: scan → Verified, in two seconds, with an audit trail.
  3. **Grievance handling** — Before: a Word doc lost in email. After: filed → escalated → resolved, timeline per case, AI-drafted first pass.
  4. **Onboarding new members** — Before: manual entry, a card in the mail weeks later. After: added to the roster, digital card live instantly.
- **Visual:** Each row: a muted "before" fragment on the left, a hairline arrow, a clean green-accented "after" tile on the right. Consistent with the entropy→order motif.

- **Visual:** A single mint-tinted "vault" panel with a hairline lock motif — calm, not alarmist. Green, not red. Trust is presented as a feature, not a fear.
