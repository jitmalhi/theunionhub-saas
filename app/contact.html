Press & speaking

For press inquiries, write to hello@theunionhub.com with "Press —" in the subject line. We respond within two business days. Speaking and panel requests welcome; we are particularly interested in conversations about how Canadian labour organizations are modernizing without sacrificing autonomy.

What not to send by email

  • Personal information about a member without authorization. Route through your union admin instead.
  • Passwords or shared secrets. If we ever need a credential rotated, we'll do it in your console — never over email.
  • Active exploits. Use security@theunionhub.com for those, and follow the disclosure guidelines.
