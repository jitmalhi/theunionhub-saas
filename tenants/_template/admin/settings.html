Settings.

Edit how this tenant appears. Only the admin email on file can save.

Tenant logo

Replaces the platform mark on member cards. PNG, JPG, SVG, or WebP, up to 5 MB. Use a square (1:1) image for the cleanest fit. Uploads happen immediately on file select; no need to click Save.

No logo

Dues cycle

How often the "already collected" check resets in the verify flow. A weekly cycle means a member scanned + marked paid on Monday shows "Already collected" through Sunday; the new week resets Monday. Changing this affects future collections only — historical rows aren't re-bucketed, so members paid in the current period under the old setting may be eligible to be marked paid again under the new setting.

Accent colour

The hex paints the union name on the digital card, the verify-screen background, and admin highlights. Must pass WCAG AA contrast against the off-white page background (#F5F4F1, ≥ 4.5:1) — same rule scripts/new-tenant.mjs enforces at provisioning time.
